### Project Overview

**WaterDash** was a real-world freelance project delivered collaboratively under the **Whimsico** brand, a micro-agency founded by myself and my good friend. Our client was a bottled spring water company seeking a Shopify-based e-commerce site to showcase and sell their product while supporting unique, data-driven customer interactions.

### Collaboration and Scope

The project was initiated when my good friend, [Maciej](https://maciejm.dev/), was approached by the business owner (a friend of his) to create a custom website. We decided to collaborate and deliver the engagement together, forming **Whimsico** as our operating identity. We worked on the project equally.

### Technical Architecture

The frontend storefront was built on **Shopify**, using the client's design language and feedback to iteratively refine the layout and user experience. My primary contribution focused on enabling the backend and deployment automation through **AWS** and CI/CD pipelines.

- **AWS Organizations:** I established a dedicated AWS Organization for WaterDash to ensure clean account separation and scalability.
- **API Gateway + Lambda:** implemented a custom serverless backend that collected user _postcodes_ and _email addresses_ from visitors interested in deliveries in areas not yet served, a unique request from the client.
- **Data Handling:** the Lambda function validated input and securely stored customer interest data for future market-expansion insights.

### Automation and CI/CD

To support seamless iteration and client feedback cycles, I developed **continuous integration and deployment pipelines** for both the Shopify theme and the AWS Lambda backend:

- Automatic deployment of theme updates to the live Shopify store when changes were merged to main.
- Automated packaging and deployment of Lambda functions via CI/CD to the client's AWS environment.
- Reduced manual deployment time to near-zero, ensuring consistent, reliable updates across systems.

### Infrastructure & Business Setup

Beyond the technical build, I also handled the foundational infrastructure for Whimsico as an operating entity:

- Registered and configured the **Whimsico domain**.
- Set up **Zoho Mail** for professional team communication.
- Provisioned the full AWS environment for both our team and the client.

### Outcome

The project was successfully delivered and deployed to production, fully meeting the client's design and technical requirements. It represented a complete **freelance production delivery**, from design collaboration to automated deployment, and served as an excellent case study in small-team execution, client management, and modern cloud-based Shopify development.

The experience of building WaterDash through Whimsico not only expanded my technical expertise in **serverless AWS architectures** and **CI/CD automation**, but also solidified my confidence in end-to-end freelance delivery, client collaboration, and business operations.
